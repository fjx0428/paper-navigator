## 问题

预训练语言模型把事实知识存在参数里，但难以精确访问和操作这些知识，在知识密集型任务上不如任务专用架构；同时，其决策缺乏可追溯来源，世界知识也难以更新。已有的参数记忆与非参数记忆混合模型（REALM、ORQA）只被用于抽取式下游任务。本文要把这种混合记忆推广到 seq2seq 生成，提出通用的检索增强生成（RAG）微调方案。

**原文依据**

> However, their ability to access and precisely manipulate knowledge is still limited, and hence on knowledge-intensive tasks, their performance lags behind task-specific architectures.

> Pre-trained models with a differentiable access mechanism to explicit non-parametric memory can overcome this issue, but have so far been only investigated for extractive downstream tasks.

> They cannot easily expand or revise their memory, can’t straightforwardly provide insight into their predictions, and may produce “hallucinations”

## 方法

RAG 由两部分组成：检索器 p(z|x) 用 DPR 双编码器在 Wikipedia 稠密向量索引上做 top-K MIPS 检索，生成器 p(y|x,z) 用 BART-large（400M 参数），把输入与检索段落拼接后生成。训练时把检索到的文档当作隐变量，用 top-K 近似做边缘化，检索器与生成器联合微调。作者给出两种形式：RAG-Sequence 对整条输出序列使用同一篇文档，RAG-Token 允许每个目标 token 依据不同文档，因此可以综合多篇文档的内容。

**原文依据**

> To train the retriever and generator end-to-end, we treat the retrieved document as a latent variable.

> In the RAG-Token model we can draw a different latent document for each target token and marginalize accordingly.

> The retrieval component pη (z|x) is based on DPR [26]. DPR follows a bi-encoder architecture

> We use BART-large [32], a pre-trained seq2seq transformer [58] with 400M parameters.

## 数据

非参数记忆是 2018 年 12 月的 Wikipedia dump，每篇文章切成不重叠的 100 词块，共 21M 篇文档，用 FAISS 建单一 MIPS 索引；训练时检索 top k，k 取 5 或 10，测试时的 k 由 dev 集决定。评测覆盖四个开放域问答数据集（Natural Questions、TriviaQA、WebQuestions、CuratedTrec，报告 Exact Match），以及 MS-MARCO NLG 抽象式问答、Jeopardy 问题生成和 FEVER 事实核查。

**原文依据**

> Each Wikipedia article is split into disjoint 100-word chunks, to make a total of 21M documents.

> Following Lee et al. [31] and Karpukhin et al. [26], we use the December 2018 dump.

> We consider k ∈ {5, 10} for training and set k for test time using dev data.

## 局限

非参数记忆被限定在 Wikipedia 单一来源，作者也承认并非所有问题都能仅靠 Wikipedia 回答，知识库本身也不可避免带有偏差。检索器并非从零任务无关地学得：它用 DPR 初始化，而 DPR 在 Natural Questions 和 TriviaQA 上接受过检索监督；作者把两个分量能否从零联合预训练列为未来工作。检索并非在所有任务上都优于词重叠基线，FEVER 上 BM25 最好；索引热替换需要重建整个索引，且论文未系统评估检索带来的延迟与算力开销。

**原文依据**

> not all questions are answerable from Wikipedia alone

> which uses retrieval supervision on Natural Questions and TriviaQA

> In future work, it may be fruitful to investigate if the two components can be jointly pre-trained from scratch

> For FEVER, BM25 performs best, perhaps since FEVER claims are heavily entity-centric and thus well-suited for word overlap-based retrieval.

> Wikipedia, or any potential external knowledge source, will probably never be entirely factual and completely devoid of bias.
